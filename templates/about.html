Methodology

What the tool does, how each number is produced, and where it can be wrong.

Pipeline

  1. Extract. Text is read from the PDF with pdfplumber. If almost no text is found, the file is treated as a scan and you are told so rather than given an empty analysis.
  2. Clean. Lower-casing, removing links, emails and phone numbers, and protecting tokens such as C++, C# and Node.js.
  3. Extract skills. A curated lexicon is matched against the text. A skill is reported only when its name or a known alias appears in the resume (or the job description).
  4. Classify. {% if metrics %}{{ metrics.selected_model }}{% else %}A TF-IDF text classifier{% endif %} predicts one of eight job categories. It was chosen over the alternative by cross-validation on training data only.
  5. Score. The suitability score below is computed against your selected role profile, or against skills found in the job description you paste.

Similarity is divided by {{ cap }} and capped at 1, so a cosine of {{ cap }} or more earns full credit. The weights and cap are judgement calls, not fitted values. With a pasted job description, every recognised skill in it counts as required and the preferred share is scored the same way as required.

Model probability vs. suitability

These are different things and are never mixed:

  • Model probability: how strongly the classifier favours the predicted category. Produced by a probabilistic classifier (calibrated if an SVM is used).
  • Suitability score: a transparent heuristic for how much of the target's requirements your resume evidences.
  • Neither is the chance of getting a job, and neither is a hiring decision.

Limits you should know about

  • A skill missing from the report may be present in your resume but phrased differently, or lost in PDF extraction. Treat "missing" as "not evidenced".
  • Multi-column and heavily designed layouts can scramble reading order and hide sections.
  • The classifier ships trained on synthetic demonstration data (see Model performance).
  • No deep learning is used. Sentence-transformer or BERT models could be added if you have enough real labelled data.
  • Privacy: the PDF is saved to a temporary file only while it is parsed, then deleted. The server stores no resumes or extracted text.
